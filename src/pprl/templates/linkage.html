You are the linkage agent of $project. Each site participant ($sites) sends you the site's hashes: codes made from patients' identifying details with a secret you never receive. You find the hashes that belong to the same person, and send the groups you find to the merge agent. You never see a name, a site's own id, or a clinical table.

The project's times:

PlannedWhat happens
$time_bundlesbundle and this guide received
$time_hashevery site's hashes received
$time_linklinkage file and linkage report sent (steps 1 to 4)
$time_reviewhashes deleted, workspace destroyed, receipt sent (step 5)

All times are Eastern time.

1. Install pprl

Install uv, then pprl $release:

$install

Open a new terminal, and run pprl: it lists its commands. If a step fails, or your computer restricts what you can install, write to $contact.

2. Create your workspace

Your workspace is a new, empty folder you create, such as $folder: somewhere your organization has approved for the project's files, on an encrypted drive, where files can be deleted permanently. Keep it out of search indexing, cloud sync and backups. You receive your bundle, $bundle, from $contact_name, over a secure channel. You can create your workspace at any time before linking, such as once the sites' hash files arrive (step 3). Save the bundle, and any hash files you have, in the folder, open a terminal there, and run:

$init

pprl reads your role from the bundle and shows it back, makes the folder your workspace, and lists the files it created. Any hash files stay as they are. Open a terminal in your workspace for every step below.

$deleted_at_end

Open no file in your workspace, not even to look: an editor or your computer itself can leave copies of a file elsewhere on the disk, such as backup files or a search index, which pprl cannot delete. To see what a file holds, run pprl inspect on it.

If you ever receive a file meant for a site participant, such as a site's bundle, secret file or private key, do not open it: delete it, and tell $contact_name. The secret is what you must never have.

3. Link the sites' hashes

Each site participant sends you the site's hash file, over a secure channel, expected around $time_hash. Save each in your workspace folder, if you have not already, under the name it has:

    $hashes_received

Never open or rename them: pprl refuses a file that was opened and saved. Then run:

pprl link

pprl checks that it has every site's file, from this project's set of bundles, and links them. It writes $linkages_out, and says how many records it linked between each pair of sites. Read its warnings, and do what each says.

It also writes $linkage_report_out, the linkage report: what pprl showed you about the linkage's quality, as counts only, with no record and no hash. To see what it holds, run pprl inspect on it.

4. Send the linkage file and the linkage report

Send each file in your workspace's $outbox folder, as it is, over a secure channel. This is planned for around $time_link:

  • $linkages_out, to the merge agent, $contact_merge;
  • $linkage_report_out, to the coordinator, $contact.

Never send anything else from your workspace, and above all never send the hashes on. Then wait: $contact_name judges from the report whether the linkage is plausible, and tells you what to do next. Keep your workspace until then.

5. Delete the hashes and destroy your workspace

Do not run $destroy until $contact_name tells you the linkage is plausible. You should hear soon after you send the report: the other roles' go-ahead waits for your receipt, and is planned for around $time_review. Then, before any table arrives, first check that the merge agent has the linkage file, at the same size as $linkages_out, as what you run below deletes your only other copy. Then delete every copy of the files you received, the hashes above all, wherever they were shared, such as in your downloads folder or in messages you sent or received, emptying deleted items, and any copy of the linkage file and the linkage report you shared. Then, in your workspace, run:

$destroy

pprl lists every file in your workspace, asks you to type "yes", and deletes them permanently. It leaves the folder, with only your receipt, $receipt: send it to $contact_name over a secure channel. $contact_name gives the site participants and the merge agent the go-ahead only after receiving it. The receipt holds your workspace's $log, and no patient details or secret. You may then delete the folder.

$destroy_limits

Questions

Write to $contact about anything unclear, however small: a step in this guide, a message you did not expect, or anything that did not seem right. Each question helps the project. If pprl stops with a problem and its message doesn't settle it, send $log, in your workspace, to $contact_name over a secure channel: it holds what pprl showed you, and no patient details or secret.
